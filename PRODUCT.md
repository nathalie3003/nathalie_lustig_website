# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two overlapping audiences, weighted equally: (1) finance recruiters and hiring managers (capital markets, private banking) evaluating Nathalie as a candidate, and (2) finance-curious peers and readers who want genuinely readable notes on bonds, rates, and macro. Visitors arrive from a shared link or search, skim the hero and latest note, and decide within seconds whether this person is credible and worth reading further.

## Product Purpose

"The Basis Point" is Nathalie Lustig's personal site: bond-market notes, an about/CV section, and project work. It exists to demonstrate genuine, specific understanding of fixed income and macro (not just credential-listing), and to double as a credibility signal for internship/full-time roles in capital markets. Success looks like a recruiter reading one note and believing the writer actually thinks about markets, and a peer bookmarking it to read the next one.

## Brand Personality

Precise, understated, serious. Reads like a research note or a well-edited publication, not a personal blog or a pitch deck. Confidence comes from restraint and specificity, not from visual noise or self-promotion.

## Anti-references

Avoid the "finance bro" / generic fintech SaaS look: glossy dashboards, gradient hero sections, hero-metric templates, stock photography of trading floors, aggressive color, marketing-site clichés (uppercase eyebrows scattered as reflexive scaffolding, numbered section markers, identical card grids). This should read as editorial/publication-like, not as a sales page for a product.

## Design Principles

- Restraint signals confidence — don't decorate; let the writing and the numbers carry weight.
- Editorial, not promotional — structure the page like a publication (mastheads, hairlines, serif type), not a landing page selling a service.
- Specific over generic — real content (yield curve, actual notes) over placeholder-shaped sections.
- Legible first — a research note that's hard to read undermines its own credibility.
- Practice what you preach — the site's own precision should mirror the analytical precision it claims to have about bond markets.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard best practice: WCAG AA contrast (body text ≥4.5:1), respect `prefers-reduced-motion`, no color-only signaling (relevant for the yield curve chart), keyboard-navigable interactive elements.
